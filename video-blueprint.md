# Nike Motion Product Film Blueprint

## Global definition

The film is a 16-second vertical 9:16 product advertisement for the Nike storefront. Its visual sub-genre is dark editorial footwear cinematography: a nearly black studio stage, sculpted side light, reflective performance materials, shallow depth of field, restrained smoke, and premium macro product photography. Rendering is photorealistic with tactile mesh, molded foam, rubber, and translucent cushioning detail. The palette is graphite black, cool silver, concrete gray, and a minimal electric-lime reflection. There is no narration and no on-screen text.

The recurring subject is the current silver Nike performance shoe: pale silver engineered mesh upper, sculpted translucent sole, cool gray overlays, white laces, and a streamlined athletic silhouette. It remains present and physically consistent throughout the film. Audio is embedded as restrained cinematic sound: low sub-bass pulse, soft air movement, subtle rubber/mesh texture, and a controlled whoosh at the final reveal.

## Clip plan

The pullback widens the frame while the same light sweep continues across the toe and heel. |

A subtle air burst and highlight roll across the sole, then the motion settles into a premium still. | The shoe remains fully present, centered-right, with the concrete floor and haze continuing. The final highlight resolves without text, logo replacement, or object disappearance. |

## Output requirements

The first keyframe must be upright 9:16. The final video should feel like a premium 16-second social product film, with one coherent shoe, controlled camera motion, detailed lighting transitions, no text overlays, and no watermarks.

## Mandatory reference image

Generate one upright white-background visual reference of the same silver Nike performance shoe in a clean three-quarter product view. This reference is for subject consistency and must not be used as the final dark video frame.
